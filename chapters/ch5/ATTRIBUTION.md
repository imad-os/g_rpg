## 2. Sound Effects
- **Kenney RPG Audio**: `audio/sfx/`
  - **Author**: Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl)
  - **Source**: https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects
  - **License**: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal)
  - **Usage**: UI clicks, door creaks, blade swings, knife draws, coin pickups, and chest lids.

- **Gothic Horror Sound Design**:
  - Vampire laughing, hissing, bat flutter, zombie groaning, bone shattering, and holy shrine chimes generated via real Web Audio parametric synthesis and sampled audio buffers with zero runtime latency.
