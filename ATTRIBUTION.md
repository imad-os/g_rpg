## 2. Chapter 6: Super Mario — Kingdoms of Wonder (`chapters/ch6/`)
- **2.5D Environment Tiles, Props, Enemies, Bosses & Costume Overlays (`chapters/ch6/art.js`, `chapters/ch6/chapter.js`, `js/hero.js`)**:
  - 100% original procedural HTML5 Canvas 2D pixel-art rendering created specifically for Hollowmere Chapter 6.
  - Includes Peach's Castle, Toadette's Royal Costume Boutique, Spotted Mushroom Houses, Warp Pipes, interactive `?` Question Blocks, Brick Blocks, Star Fountain, 23 enemy archetypes, 6 Kingdom Bosses (including 3-Phase Bowser), Super Mushroom items, and the Mario, Luigi, and Princess Peach Cosmetic Costume Overlays.
  - License: `CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)`.
- **Chapter 6 Chiptune Music & Sound Effects (`js/audio.js`, `chapters/ch6/chapter.js`)**:
  - 100% original procedural WebAudio API synthesized compositions (`c6_village`, `c6_meadow`, `c6_jungle`, `c6_haunted`, `c6_frost`, `c6_sky`, `c6_darkfire`, `c6_boss`, `c6_bowser1`, `c6_bowser2`, `c6_bowser3`) and synthesized SFX (`jump`, `mushroom`, `powerdown`, `fireball`, `shell`, `boo`, `star`, `rescue`, `pipe`, `qblock`).
